## Overview

**Mezzo** is a premier, high-fidelity music streaming application designed for true audiophiles and music lovers. Built to deliver millions of songs with pristine acoustic clarity, Mezzo pairs lossless audio resolution with an ultra-responsive, distraction-free interface.

Whether you're streaming the latest global chart-toppers on your phone or enjoying high-res playback through studio headphones on your desktop, Mezzo delivers an uncompromising, subscription-free music experience without audio advertisements or paywalls.

## Key Features

### 🎧 Lossless Audio Quality
- Stream tracks in uncompressed, high-bitrate studio quality (including Lossless FLAC and 24-bit Hi-Res).
- Experience deep bass dynamics, rich vocal clarity, and wide soundstage separation.

### ⚡ Pure & Completely Ad-Free
- No audio commercials, sponsor interruptions, or forced breaks between your tracks.
- Enjoy uninterrupted queue playback from start to finish.

### 📱 Native Android Application
- **Ultra-lightweight**: Fast download and installation at only **~3.4 MB**.
- **System Integration**: Seamless background playback, lock screen media artwork, and notification drawer controls.
- **Battery-optimized**: Smooth, power-efficient native hardware acceleration.

### 🎙️ Synchronized Real-Time Lyrics
- Sing along with accurate, time-synced lyrics that automatically scroll with the artist's vocals.
- View full lyric sheets or karaoke-style lines with smooth typographic highlighting.

### 🎚️ 10-Band Studio Precision Equalizer
- Sculpt your acoustic curve with a dedicated 10-band equalizer (32Hz to 16kHz).
- One-tap audio presets: **Bass Boost**, **Vocal Booster**, **Rock**, **Pop**, **Electronic**, **Acoustic**, **Classical**, and **Lo-Fi**.

### 💾 Smart Offline Music Storage
- Save albums, playlists, and personal favorites directly to local device storage.
- Keep listening seamlessly during flights, underground commutes, or remote travels without an internet connection.

### 🌐 Cross-Platform Web Player
- Instant browser playback on **macOS**, **Windows**, **Linux**, and **iOS**.
- Full Progressive Web App (PWA) support — add to your home screen or desktop for a standalone native app feel.

### 🔒 Personalized Onboarding & Discovery
- Tailored home feed curated to your favorite languages (Hindi, Punjabi, South Indian, English, Global Hits, and more) and artists.
- Universal Google One-Tap authentication and email login with instant cross-device playlist sync.
